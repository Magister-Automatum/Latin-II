Bridge — Neuters (Stage 7)
The neuter problem: a neuter noun looks the same as S and as DO (signum, ōmen, perīculum). The ending alone cannot tell you the role — check the other nouns and the verb. Plural = -a (signa, ōmina).
Characters: Grumio — the cook, now in Rome  |  Cerberus — his dog  |  astrologus — a fortune-teller  |  senātor — an important Roman  |  corvus — a raven
