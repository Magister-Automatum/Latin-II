Iter Grumiōnis · I

Review for Stage 24 — The PPP
The PPP: the 4th principal part (scrībō… scrīptus) used as an ADJECTIVE — "having been written." It rides a noun and copies its ending: epistula scrīpta, epistulam scrīptam, verba scrīpta. Don't confuse it with the perfect VERB (scrīpsit — he wrote).
Characters: Grumio — the cook, once a boy in Greece  |  Cerberus — his dog  |  Nīcander — a Greek wine-merchant  |  vīcīna — the widow next door  |  senex — the letter's unseen writer
