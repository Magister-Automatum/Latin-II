Review for Stage 24 — Perfect Passive
The perfect passive: PPP + esse = a full verb. dēlētus est — was destroyed · āmissa sunt — were lost · captus erat — HAD been taken. The participle agrees with the SUBJECT. Active tells who did it (dēlēvit); passive faces what it was done to.
Characters: Grumio — the cook, sailing home  |  Cerberus — his dog  |  Nīcander — merchant and captain  |  vidua — a Greek widow aboard  |  fīlia — her small daughter  |  nautae — the crew
