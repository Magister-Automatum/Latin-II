Bridge — Perfect Tense (Stage 8)
The perfect tense: a completed, one-time action — a snapshot, not a video. Look for -vit / -vērunt (labōrāvit) or a changed stem from the 3rd principal part (cēpit, fuit, iit, dīxit). Ongoing background uses the imperfect (-bat).
Characters: Grumio — the cook  |  Cerberus — his dog  |  vīlicus — the farm overseer  |  ancilla — an enslaved woman  |  custōs — the night guard  |  equī — the horses
