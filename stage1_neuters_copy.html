Caecilius et Ātrium

Stage 1 · Neutra (2nd decl.)
The neuter rule: a neuter noun wears -um as both subject and direct object. You cannot tell from the ending alone — check the other nouns and the verb. Plural neuters end in -a (ātria, cubicula, vīna).
Characters: Caecilius — the banker  |  Metella — his wife  |  Quintus — their son  |  Grumio — the cook  |  Cerberus — the dog
